1  ISO 19011:2026 – Assessment MCQs  Q1. What is the main purpose of ISO 19011:2026?  A. To specify product quality requirements B. To provide guidelines for auditing management systems C. To establish environmental performance limits D. To certify individual auditors  Answer: B. To provide guidelines for auditing management systems  Q2. Which of the following is an auditing principle in ISO 19011:2026?  A. Profitability B. Confidentiality C. Production efficiency D. Product design  Answer: B. Confidentiality  Q3. What does the principle of integrity require from an auditor?  A. To report findings honestly and ethically B. To change evidence to support the audit conclusion C. To avoid documenting nonconformities D. To guarantee that no problems will be found  Answer: A. To report findings honestly and ethically  Q4. What is the purpose of an audit programme?  A. To manage one employee's daily tasks B. To organize one or more audits over a defined period and toward specific objectives C. To replace management review D. To approve all operational purchases  Answer: B. To organize one or more audits over a defined period and toward specific objectives  Q5. Which factor should be considered when determining audit programme risks and opportunities?  A. The auditor's personal preferences B. The context of the organization and factors that may affect the audit programme C. The colour of audit documents D. The number of office visitors  Answer: B. The context of the organization and factors that may affect the audit programme  Q6. What should be defined before conducting an individual audit?  A. Audit objectives, scope and criteria B. Employee salary levels C. Product selling prices D. The organization's advertising plan  Answer: A. Audit objectives, scope and criteria
