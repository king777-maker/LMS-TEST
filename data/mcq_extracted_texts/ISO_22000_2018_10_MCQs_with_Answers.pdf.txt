1  ISO 22000:2018 – Assessment MCQs  Q1. What is the main purpose of ISO 22000:2018?  A. To establish requirements for an environmental management system B. To provide requirements for a Food Safety Management System (FSMS) C. To establish requirements for occupational health and safety D. To define financial management procedures  Answer: B. To provide requirements for a Food Safety Management System (FSMS)  Q2. Which organizations can use ISO 22000:2018?  A. Only food manufacturing companies B. Only restaurants and hotels C. Any organization directly or indirectly involved in the food chain D. Only government food inspection agencies  Answer: C. Any organization directly or indirectly involved in the food chain  Q3. What does HACCP stand for?  A. Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points B. Health Assessment and Control Compliance Programme C. Hazard Audit and Certification Control Process D. Hygiene Analysis and Critical Cleaning Procedures  Answer: A. Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points  Q4. What is the purpose of prerequisite programmes (PRPs)?  A. To establish basic conditions and activities needed to maintain a hygienic environment B. To replace the HACCP plan C. To determine product selling prices D. To conduct employee performance reviews  Answer: A. To establish basic conditions and activities needed to maintain a hygienic environment  Q5. Which of the following is an example of a biological food safety hazard?  A. Glass fragments B. Cleaning chemical residue C. Salmonella bacteria D. Metal particles  Answer: C. Salmonella bacteria  Q6. What is a Critical Control Point (CCP)?  A. A step where control can be applied and is essential to prevent, eliminate or reduce a significant food safety hazard to an acceptable level B. Any routine office activity C. A point used only for product packaging design D. A stage where food safety monitoring is unnecessary  Answer: A. A step where control can be applied and is essential to prevent, eliminate or reduce a significant food safety hazard to an acceptable level

2  Q7. What is the purpose of establishing critical limits at a CCP?  A. To define measurable or observable criteria that separate acceptable from unacceptable control B. To set employee working hours C. To determine the product's market price D. To eliminate the need for monitoring  Answer: A. To define measurable or observable criteria that separate acceptable from unacceptable control  Q8. What should an organization do when monitoring shows that a CCP is not under control?  A. Ignore the result if the product looks acceptable B. Take appropriate corrections and corrective actions according to the established procedures C. Continue production without recording the issue D. Delete the monitoring records  Answer: B. Take appropriate corrections and corrective actions according to the established procedures  Q9. Why is traceability important in a Food Safety Management System?  A. It helps identify and trace food, ingredients and relevant information through the food chain B. It replaces supplier evaluation C. It removes the need for product identification D. It is used only for financial reporting  Answer: A. It helps identify and trace food, ingredients and relevant information through the food chain  Q10. What is the purpose of verification in ISO 22000:2018?  A. To confirm that planned arrangements are implemented and effective B. To advertise food products C. To replace validation of control measures D. To avoid reviewing FSMS performance  Answer: A. To confirm that planned arrangements are implemented and effective
