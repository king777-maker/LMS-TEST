1  7 QC Tools – Assessment MCQs  Q1. What is the primary purpose of the 7 QC Tools?  A. To increase production costs B. To identify, analyze and solve quality problems C. To replace quality inspections D. To reduce employee training  Answer: B. To identify, analyze and solve quality problems  Q2. Which 7 QC Tool is used to identify and prioritize the most significant problems?  A. Histogram B. Scatter Diagram C. Pareto Chart D. Control Chart  Answer: C. Pareto Chart  Q3. Which tool is commonly used to identify possible causes of a problem?  A. Cause-and-Effect Diagram B. Check Sheet C. Histogram D. Control Chart  Answer: A. Cause-and-Effect Diagram  Q4. What is the main purpose of a Check Sheet?  A. To display process stability B. To collect and record data systematically C. To show relationships between two variables D. To rank suppliers  Answer: B. To collect and record data systematically  Q5. Which tool displays the frequency distribution of numerical data?  A. Histogram B. Flowchart C. Pareto Chart D. Scatter Diagram  Answer: A. Histogram  Q6. What does a Scatter Diagram help determine?  A. The sequence of process steps B. The relationship between two variables C. The frequency of defects only D. The root cause with certainty  Answer: B. The relationship between two variables

2  Q7. Which tool is used to monitor process performance over time and identify variation?  A. Check Sheet B. Histogram C. Control Chart D. Cause-and-Effect Diagram  Answer: C. Control Chart  Q8. What is the main purpose of a Flowchart?  A. To show the steps and sequence of a process B. To calculate process capability C. To rank defects by frequency D. To measure customer satisfaction  Answer: A. To show the steps and sequence of a process  Q9. In a Pareto Chart, what does the 80/20 principle generally suggest?  A. All causes contribute equally B. A small number of causes often account for a large proportion of problems C. 80% of data must be rejected D. Every problem has exactly two causes  Answer: B. A small number of causes often account for a large proportion of problems  Q10. Which of the following is the correct set of seven basic QC tools?  A. 5S, Kaizen, FMEA, SPC, TPM, TQM and 8D B. Check Sheet, Histogram, Pareto Chart, Cause-and-Effect Diagram, Scatter Diagram, Control Chart and Stratification/Graph C. SWOT, PESTLE, SIPOC, DMAIC, 5 Whys, FMEA and Gantt Chart D. Audit, Inspection, Calibration, Sampling, Benchmarking, Training and Reporting  Answer: B. Check Sheet, Histogram, Pareto Chart, Cause-and-Effect Diagram, Scatter Diagram, Control Chart and Stratification/Graph
