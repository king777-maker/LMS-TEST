Page 1  ISO 9001:2026 – Assessment MCQs  10 Multiple-Choice Questions with Answers  Instructions:   Each question carries 1 mark. Select the correct option. Suggested passing score: 7 out of 10 (70%).  Assessment Questions and Answers  Q1. What is the primary purpose of ISO 9001:2026?  A. To establish environmental management requirements B. To establish requirements for a Quality Management System C. To establish occupational health and safety requirements D. To establish financial management requirements  Answer: B. To establish requirements for a Quality Management System  Q2. Which organization publishes the ISO 9001 standard?  A. International Labour Organization (ILO) B. World Trade Organization (WTO) C. International Organization for Standardization (ISO) D. World Health Organization (WHO)  Answer: C. International Organization for Standardization (ISO)  Q3. Which of the following is emphasized more strongly in ISO 9001:2026?  A. Financial auditing B. Quality culture and ethical behaviour C. Employee salary management D. Marketing strategies  Answer: B. Quality culture and ethical behaviour  Q4. What is the main responsibility of top management in a Quality Management System?  A. Delegate all quality responsibilities to the quality department B. Demonstrate leadership and commitment to the QMS C. Perform all internal audits personally D. Approve every purchase order  Answer: B. Demonstrate leadership and commitment to the QMS  Q5. What is the main objective of customer focus in ISO 9001:2026?  A. Increasing employee working hours B. Reducing product variety C. Enhancing customer satisfaction and meeting customer requirements D. Reducing communication with customers  Answer: C. Enhancing customer satisfaction and meeting customer requirements  Q6. How does ISO 9001:2026 address risks and opportunities?  A. By ignoring potential risks B. By addressing only financial risks C. By providing a clearer distinction between actions to address risks and opportunities D. By eliminating the need for risk assessment  Answer: C. By providing a clearer distinction between actions to address risks and opportunities

What is the purpose of an internal audit?  A. To identify employees for termination B. To evaluate whether the QMS conforms to requirements and is effectively implemented C. What is the purpose of continual improvement in a QMS?  A. To maintain existing processes without changes B. To reduce customer interaction C. To improve the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of the QMS D. To eliminate the need for performance evaluation  Answer: C. To improve the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of the QMS  Q10. What is the purpose of Annex A in ISO 9001:2026?  A. To provide financial reporting formats B. To clarify key concepts, terminology and the intent of requirements C. To establish employee attendance rules D. To provide product pricing guidelines  Answer: B. To clarify key concepts, terminology and the intent of requirements  Note:   These are general knowledge-assessment questions prepared for LMS use.
