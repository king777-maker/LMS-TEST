Section 1: Questions and Answers  Q1. What is the primary purpose of the 5S methodology?  A. To increase employee working hours B. To create a clean, organized, safe and efficient workplace C. To reduce employee responsibilities D. To increase paperwork  Correct Answer: B. To create a clean, organized, safe and efficient workplace  Q2. What does the first S – Seiri – mean?  A. Sort B. Set in Order C. Shine D. Sustain  Correct Answer: A. Sort  Q3. What is the main objective of Seiton (Set in Order)?  A. Remove unnecessary items B. Clean the workplace C. Arrange necessary items so they are easy to find and use D. Conduct employee training  Correct Answer: C. Arrange necessary items so they are easy to find and use  Q4. What does Seiso (Shine) focus on?  A. Cleaning and inspecting the workplace regularly B. Increasing production targets C. Removing all workplace documents D. Changing employee responsibilities  Correct Answer: A. Cleaning and inspecting the workplace regularly  Q5. What is the purpose of Seiketsu (Standardize)?  A. To introduce new machines B. To establish standards to maintain the first three S activities C. To reduce employee participation D. To eliminate workplace inspections  Correct Answer: B. To establish standards to maintain the first three S activities

Keeping unnecessary materials at the workstation B. Removing unused tools and materials from the workplace C. Cleaning the production floor D. Labelling storage locations  Correct Answer: B. To make workplace conditions, locations and abnormalities easy to identify C. To increase the number of reports D. To replace employee training  Correct Answer: B. Reduced workplace efficiency C. Improved productivity, safety and quality D. Increased searching time  Correct Answer: C. Improved productivity, safety and quality  Q10. Who is responsible for maintaining 5S in an organization?  A. Only the housekeeping department B. Only the quality manager C. Only top management D. All employees, with management support  Correct Answer: D.
